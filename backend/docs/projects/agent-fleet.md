# Agent Fleet

## Overview
My own working system of more than 40 AI agents, running on Grok Bot. One head agent takes my requests and hands each one to the group that fits. The groups do the work and report back, and I get a single summary. Anything that goes out to the world waits for my approval. I use it every day, and it is the hands-on experience behind the agents I build for clients. The setup is private.

## Technical Details
- **Platform**: Grok Bot
- **Structure**: One head agent, group leads, and specialised agents under each lead
- **Control**: A person approves every outward action

## Key Features
- One way in: every request goes to the head agent, which decides which group handles it
- Groups by field: video production, content drafts, collecting and analysing activity on professional networks, monitoring the cost, health and security of cloud infrastructure, market research, knowledge and memory, and the health of the agents themselves
- Clear reporting: an agent reports to its group lead, the lead reports to the head agent, and I receive one message instead of dozens
- Permissions by role: each agent gets only the access its job needs, and several groups can only read and never change anything
- A person decides: nothing is sent, published, deployed or changed outside the system without my approval. Agents write drafts, and I publish myself
- Agents that watch agents: one group checks that the others are working and keeps the map of the system up to date

## How it is built
I talk to one head agent, by chat or by voice. It passes each request to one of seven groups: cloud and infrastructure (3 agents under a lead), fleet reliability (3), freelance business (5), LinkedIn (3), X (3), Trinity Legal: the next step (4) and a video studio (6). Seven more agents report straight to the head agent: content drafts, tasks, the business's paperwork and the fleet's memory. My personal memory is open to one agent only, read-only. Each agent reports to its lead, each lead to the head agent, and I receive one summary.

## Examples from daily work
- A short video end to end: the studio lead writes a plan and a script, other agents make images, narration and sound, a quality check runs midway and again on the full video, and it goes out only after I approve.
- Market research for Trinity Legal: public sources only, with a source for every number; 28 fitting offices and 22 outreach drafts, none sent without my approval.
- Before 23 likes on LinkedIn, the agent stopped and asked me instead of acting alone.
- Every morning a summary, every evening a LinkedIn activity run, and every 12 hours a check of changes and health.

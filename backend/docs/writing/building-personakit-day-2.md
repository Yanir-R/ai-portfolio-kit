# Building PersonaKit: Day 2

AI assistants are starting to remember us.

That solves a real problem.

Until now, every conversation began from zero. You had to repeat your work, preferences, projects, writing style, constraints, and personal context again and again.

Memory changes that.

It lets work continue across sessions.

- A conversation can become a handoff.
- A decision can become context for the next agent.
- A product discussion can become a ticket validation checklist.
- A previous debugging session can shape the next one without being pasted again from scratch.

That is the promise.

But it also introduces a more important question:

Who decides what becomes memory?

Once something enters an assistant’s context, it does more than sit there.

It shapes recommendations.
It influences how your intent is interpreted.
It creates assumptions about what you want.
It can follow you into conversations where it no longer belongs.

So I do not think the next layer is simply more memory.

It is accountable memory.

Memory you can inspect.
Memory connected to its source.
Memory you can correct or remove.
Memory you can restrict by context.
Memory you can take across assistants.

That is the direction I am taking with PersonaKit.

We are not trying to compete with assistants on who can remember more.

We are building the layer that lets people decide what their assistants are allowed to remember, trust, and reuse.

If AI memory is going to shape our decisions, people should be able to shape the memory.

Follow along as I build it.
